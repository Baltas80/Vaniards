# Vaniards — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026

Vaniards is a 2D game and does not require the player to create an account.

In the current project configuration, Vaniards does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera content or microphone recordings.

The anonymous GDevelop metrics event present in the original example has been removed from the adapted project.

The current project does not include advertising, in-app purchases, Firebase authentication, leaderboards or other online account features.

## Third-party services

Vaniards is built with GDevelop and uses third-party assets such as graphics, fonts and music. Those assets retain their own licenses and attribution requirements. Their inclusion does not mean that Vaniards sends personal data to their authors.

## Future changes

This policy must be reviewed and updated before releasing any version that adds advertising, analytics, online services, accounts, purchases or another feature that processes user data.
