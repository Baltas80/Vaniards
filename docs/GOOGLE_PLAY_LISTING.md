# Google Play Store listing — Vaniards

## English (default)

**App name**

Vaniards

**Short description**

Fight through a dark fantasy world with sword, dash and deadly enemies.

**Full description**

Vaniards is a 2D action-platformer built around classic side-scrolling combat.

Run, jump, attack and dash through dangerous areas filled with traps and enemies. Manage your health and stamina, reach checkpoints and face boss encounters.

Features:
- 2D action-platforming.
- Sword combat.
- Jump and dash mechanics with stamina.
- Multiple enemy types.
- Checkpoints and lives.
- Boss encounter.
- Keyboard and gamepad support.
- Mobile touch controls.
- English interface by default.
- Spanish language option.

Vaniards is designed for landscape play on Android devices.
