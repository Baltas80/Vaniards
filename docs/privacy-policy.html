Vaniards — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026

Information collection

Vaniards is a 2D game and does not require an account. In the current release configuration, Vaniards does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, camera content, or microphone recordings.

Analytics

The anonymous GDevelop metrics event included in the original example has been removed from the Vaniards project.

Third-party services

The current project does not include advertising, in-app purchases, Firebase authentication, leaderboards, or other online account features.

Vaniards is built with GDevelop and includes third-party assets such as graphics, fonts, music and sound effects. Those assets retain their own licenses and attribution requirements.

Future versions

This policy will be reviewed before any version adds advertising, analytics, online services, accounts, purchases, or another feature that processes user data.
